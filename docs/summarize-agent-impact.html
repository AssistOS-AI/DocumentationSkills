Skill reference

Summarize agent impact

Explains an agent's important decision or completed project outcome in terms a reader can understand without knowing the implementation.

When to use it

Use it after a decision that changes the work's direction or after a material change to a feature, user behavior, or operation. Combine related actions into one summary. Routine commands, minor edits, and repeated progress notes do not need one.

Output

The agent adds a short paragraph in the user's language between two identical <<summarize-agent-impact>> text markers. The second marker has no slash. The paragraph explains what was decided or changed and why it matters. It states material limits and distinguishes a decision from completed implementation. Code details and test logs stay outside the block.
